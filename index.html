About Mohammed Sufiyan

Mohammed Sufiyan is a freelance Web Designer, Web Developer, and Graphic Designer based in Bangalore, India. He specializes in building responsive websites, crafting modern user interfaces, and designing clean visual brand identities.

Web Design & Graphic Design Services

He works remotely with startups, businesses, and individuals globally, delivering high-quality web development, UI/UX design, and comprehensive branding solutions. His work relies heavily on a clean, Swiss-style minimalistic approach focusing on performance and usability.

Portfolio and Projects

This portfolio showcases real-world projects, including custom website design, frontend development, logo creation, and comprehensive brand identity systems. Each project demonstrates practical skills in modern web technologies and user-centered graphic design.
